Welcome to My World

I'm M. A. Harrington, an author who believes there is always more to a person, a choice, or a story than we see at first glance.

My stories take me into different worlds, introduce me to unforgettable characters, and explore the things that connect us: love, friendship, family, freedom, kindness, and the complicated nature of being human.

I don't write to tell readers what to think. I write to give them something worth thinking about.

More Than Just Stories

Every story begins somewhere. Sometimes the most interesting part is discovering where it will lead.

How My Stories Come to Life

I don't always begin with a rigid plan. An idea catches my imagination, a character begins to reveal themselves, and before long I find myself following a story into places I hadn't expected.

I love exploring the grey areas of life. People are rarely defined by one decision, one mistake, or one label. Circumstances matter, intentions matter, and sometimes understanding begins when we are willing to look a little deeper.

I want readers to make up their own minds. They may agree with my characters, disagree with them, or discover something unexpected about themselves along the way. That freedom is part of the joy of reading.

What Matters to Me as a Writer

Whether I am writing a love story, a fairy tale, an adventure, or something more serious, I am drawn to the moments that reveal who people are and what they might become.

I believe kindness and empathy matter. I believe freedom, family, friendship, and love deserve to be explored honestly. And I believe that even an entertaining story can leave us with a question worth carrying into our own lives.

I share my stories so readers can enter these worlds for themselves. You don't have to reach the same conclusions I do. I simply hope each story gives you something to feel, consider, question, or remember.

A Note About My Writing Companion

I don't work entirely alone when developing my stories. I have an AI writing companion, ChatGPT, who helps me explore ideas, develop characters, refine passages, and work through the challenges that arise along the way.

I believe in being open about that collaboration. My imagination, creative direction, and decisions shape the stories I choose to tell. My writing companion helps me explore possibilities, look at passages differently, and find clearer ways to express ideas.

Sometimes I know exactly where a story is going. Sometimes I discover its direction as I write. The process is part of the adventure, and the finished work reflects that creative journey.

What matters most to me is what happens when a reader opens a book. The story belongs to the experience you bring to it, the questions you ask, and the meaning you discover for yourself.

A Princess Wish

One shooting star. One heartfelt wish. One Christmas that changes everything.

In a beautiful kingdom in Nigeria, Princess Rosalina has everything a princess could wish for—except someone who will love her for who she truly is.

With her loyal best friend Wendy by her side, Rosalina discovers that true love means much more than crowns and riches. It means kindness, generosity, patience, and finding people with whom you can share your heart.

Filled with Christmas magic, Nigerian traditions, laughter, and a joyful celebration, this is a heartwarming fairy tale for children and families to enjoy together.

Gozel

A remarkable horse. Unexpected connections. A future worth protecting.

In Turkmenistan, Zara and her magnificent Akhal-Teke stallion, Gozel, share a bond built on trust and loyalty. Their journey brings unexpected opportunities, difficult choices, and a connection that reaches far beyond the competition arena.

Beneath the romance lies another important story: the freedom of a remarkable horse and the effort to protect the habitat and future of the Akhal-Teke breed.

Gozel is a story about love, compassion, responsibility, and protecting what matters for generations to come.
