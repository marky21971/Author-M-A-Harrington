👑 A Princess Wish

One shooting star. One heartfelt wish. One Christmas that changes everything.

Once upon a time, in a beautiful kingdom in Nigeria, Princess Rosalina had everything a princess could wish for—except someone who would love her for who she truly was.

Every Christmas Eve, Rosalina looks to the stars, hoping her special wish will one day come true. With her loyal best friend Wendy by her side, she prepares a magical Christmas filled with sparkling lights, delicious food, laughter, and festive surprises.

But when a handsome young prince enters her life, Rosalina discovers that true love is about much more than crowns and riches. It is about kindness, patience, generosity, and finding a family where love can flourish.

Filled with Christmas magic, Nigerian traditions, playful adventures, romance, and a joyful wedding celebration, A Princess Wish is a heartwarming fairy tale for children and families to enjoy together.

Because sometimes, the wish that comes true is even more wonderful than the one we imagined.

🐎 Gozel

A beautiful horse. A determined young woman. And a bond that brings two worlds together.

In Turkmenistan, Zara and her magnificent Akhal-Teke stallion, Gozel, share a special connection built on trust, loyalty, and love. When they enter a spectacular Nowruz horse-dance competition, Zara hopes their partnership will shine before the crowd.

Their success brings an unexpected opportunity when horse breeder Rizal proposes breeding Gozel with his beautiful mare, Jamila. For Zara's family, the offer could make a real difference—but the decision is about far more than money.

As two remarkable horses begin to form a bond of their own, Zara and Rizal discover that life can bring unexpected friendships, difficult choices, and possibilities neither of them imagined.

Sometimes, the most beautiful connections begin when we open our hearts to the unexpected.
